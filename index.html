Talk with Leo

2 minutes · voice or text

A short interactive simulation. Leo is 9. He handed in a book report a chatbot wrote for him — and he is waiting to find out whether he is in trouble.

Before you start

You are Ms. Rivera, Leo's 4th-grade teacher. It is just after class. Leo is sitting with you, and you have 2 minutes to talk with him about the report.

Your job: find out why he used the chatbot — without making him shut down. Ask him what happened, in his words. See what he tells you when he feels safe, and what he hides when he doesn't.

Watch his face. Leo's expression changes with how he feels — nervous, thinking, shutting down — and his mouth moves while he talks. His mouth follows the sound of his voice, not the exact words.

Checking the connection…

Voices come from ElevenLabs through the site's Netlify functions. Pick one that sounds young. If no voice loads, Leo will use your browser's built-in voice instead — or pick "Text only" and read his replies.

Stored only in this browser's localStorage, sent only to the provider you chose.

How this works & privacy

When you talk, your words go to the AI model playing Leo (through a Netlify function, so no key lives in this page) and his replies come back as text and as spoken audio. Nothing is stored on the server. The conversation stays in this tab unless you download the transcript at the end. If your browser doesn't offer a microphone, you can type instead — Leo answers the same way.

Leo is a fictional illustrated character, not a real child. His voice is a synthetic voice, not a child's voice.
